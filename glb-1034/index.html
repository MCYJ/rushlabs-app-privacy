Privacy policy

App reference: glb-1034
Updated November 3, 2026

This policy covers this paid app and its included local study features. No RushLabs account is required.

Information on your device

The app stores answer history, bookmarks, study progress, study preferences and optional local quiz notification settings locally on your device.

The Contractor Cost and Schedule Workbook keeps the cost, margin, duration, starting time and availability assumptions you enter, together with any workbook input sets you explicitly save, on your device. Workbook calculations are learning exercises; RushLabs does not receive them.

Study content is included in the app. Studying does not require a RushLabs server connection.

Information we do not collect

RushLabs does not collect or receive your answers, bookmarks, study records or notification content. The app does not upload your contacts, precise location, photos or personal files to RushLabs. This release has no advertising or third-party behavioral analytics.

This policy page uses no cookies, advertising or analytics. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process ordinary connection information under GitHub’s privacy statement. Opening an external official-source or support link is subject to that destination’s privacy policy.

Purchases

Apple or Google handles the app purchase and download through your store account. Included study content does not require an additional subscription or in-app purchase. This release has no in-app purchase or restore flow and sends no purchase receipts to RushLabs. Store payment information is handled by the store.

Optional local notifications

Quiz reminders are scheduled on your device only after you enable them and grant the required device permission. Question text is hidden by default. It appears in notifications only after your explicit opt-in. You can disable reminders or hide question text in the app and manage notification permissions in device settings.

Retention, backups and deletion

Local study information stays on your device until you remove it. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes its local storage, subject to your device’s backup and restore behavior. Apple or Google retains store purchase history separately.

You can delete saved workbook input sets in the workbook. Unsaved changes are a separate working draft. Do not assume a general study export contains saved workbook input sets unless the app explicitly identifies them in that export.

Operating-system backups may retain a copy of app data according to your backup settings. Any file you choose to export or share is under your control; delete separately saved copies when no longer needed. RushLabs does not host your study backups.

Study scope

This is an independent study tool. Workbook percentages, prices and schedule assumptions are supplied by the learner or by the included exercise. They are not submitted to a licensing authority, insurer, employer or tax authority.
