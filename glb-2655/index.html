Pair Acting Grade 1 Study

Privacy policy

App reference: glb-2655

Study records on your device

RushLabs provides Pair Acting Grade 1 Study. The study app uses no account. It saves your practice answers, progress, bookmarks, rehearsal notebook entries and app preferences locally on your device. The app code does not send these study records to RushLabs or use them for advertising or analytics.

Reminders and device backups

If you enable a reminder and grant notification permission, the app schedules it through your device. You can change reminder settings in the app or disable notifications in your system settings. Your operating system may include local app data in backups or device transfers according to your settings.

Purchases and external links

App marketplaces process the app purchase under their own policies. The app includes the study content with the paid download, without a separate in-app subscription. Opening an official resource, help page or other external link can send ordinary browsing information to the site you choose to visit. Its own privacy policy governs that visit.

Support and deletion

If you contact RushLabs for support, we receive the information you send and use it to respond. You can remove the app and its local data through your device settings. Copies in system backups are managed through your device or backup provider.
