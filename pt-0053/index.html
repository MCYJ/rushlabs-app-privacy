Oita, Beppu & Yufuin Travel Privacy Policy

RushLabs publishes this Pocket Travel app for Oita, Beppu and Yufuin. You can browse the guide and plan your itinerary without creating an account.

Information on your device

The app stores itinerary choices and settings, such as language and appearance, on your device. RushLabs does not operate a server that receives or syncs this app’s itineraries. You can remove stops in the app or remove its local data by deleting the app from your device.

Map and location

When you choose to centre the map on your location, the app asks for permission to access your device location while you use the app. The coordinates are used to centre the map and show your position. You can decline or change location permission in device settings.

If your copy has an Offline mode switch (iOS 1.0.0 builds 1 and 2), then in online map mode, the app requests map tiles directly from OpenStreetMap. The tile service receives request information such as your IP address, app identifier and the tile coordinates for the map area being viewed. If you centre the online map on your location, the tiles requested can indicate that area. The app does not include your device’s raw GPS coordinates in the tile request. The OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy explains its handling of requests.

Offline map mode uses bundled map data instead of making online map tile requests. External maps and websites can still require an internet connection when you choose to open them.

If you use the bundled-map-only release (iOS 1.0.0 build 3), the in-app map for the covered Oita region reads map data bundled with the app on your device. It does not request map tiles from a network service, even while your device is online. If you choose to centre that map on your location, the app uses your position on your device without sending it to RushLabs or a map tile provider.

When you choose an attribution link, a destination website or external directions, the external service may need internet access and process your request under its own policies.

External directions

When you choose external directions on iOS, the app passes the selected destination’s coordinates and label to Apple Maps. Apple handles that request under its Maps privacy notice. Information you enter in an external app or website is handled by that service.

Support

If you contact us through the support page, we use the information you choose to share to respond to your request. Please avoid including sensitive or unnecessary travel details.
