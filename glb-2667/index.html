Privacy policy

RushLabs develops Dry Stone Walling L1 Study. This policy describes the study information the app uses.

Study information on your device

The app keeps study activity and preferences in local storage so you can return to your work. This can include answers, bookmarks, progress and settings. Device backups depend on your operating system and its settings.

Notifications, links and purchases

Lock Quiz notifications are optional. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings. If you enable study text on the lock screen, people who can see your screen may be able to read it.

If you open a reference or support link, the external service handles that visit under its own privacy policy. Apple processes App Store purchases under its own terms. When you contact support, RushLabs uses the information you provide to answer your request.
