Musical Theatre Entry

Privacy policy

App reference: glb-2615
Updated November 3, 2026

This policy covers Musical Theatre Entry, a paid independent study app for selected LAMDA Entry Level Musical Theatre topics, including the One-Song Character Notebook. No RushLabs account is required.

Information on your device

The app stores answer history, bookmarks, study progress, study activity and optional local quiz reminder settings in local app storage on your device. Study content is included in the app; normal study does not require a RushLabs server connection.

The One-Song Character Notebook stores the rehearsal material you explicitly save, including notebook names, song references, character and rehearsal plans, dated versions, and linked rehearsal observations. Avoid entering sensitive personal information or copying lyrics.

These are user-entered rehearsal notes, not independently verified performance results. The app does not record audio, video or physical movement. Use fictional role names where possible and avoid personal or health information in your notes.

Saving, loading and deleting the notebook

Save added records stores up to six named notebooks on this device. Add or clear any pending form drafts before saving. Opening the tool does not automatically load a saved notebook. Select a saved notebook and use Load selected notebook to replace the current notebook in memory. The app blocks loading while pending form drafts remain.

Delete selected saved notebook removes only the selected saved copy. The current notebook stays in memory. Starting a blank notebook clears the current working notebook and keeps the saved copies. These actions do not remove study answers or bookmarks.

The app stores notebooks locally. It does not provide notebook file import, export, cloud synchronisation or sharing with other learners.

Information we do not collect

RushLabs does not collect or receive your answers, bookmarks, study records, notebook notes or notification content through this app. The app provides no study-data upload service and does not upload contacts, precise location, photos or personal files to RushLabs. This release has no advertising or third-party behavioral analytics.

This policy page contains no advertising, analytics scripts or forms. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process ordinary connection information under GitHub’s privacy statement. Opening an official-source, terms or support link is subject to that destination’s privacy policy.

Purchases

Apple or Google handles the app purchase and download through your store account. One paid download provides the included study content and local notebook without an additional subscription or in-app purchase. RushLabs does not receive your payment-card details or purchase receipts. Store payment information and purchase history are handled by the store.

Optional local notifications

Quiz reminders are off by default. They are scheduled on your device after you enable them and grant the required device permission. Question text is hidden by default and appears in notifications only after your explicit opt-in. The app uses your device’s time-zone setting to schedule reminders locally. You can disable reminders, hide question text and manage notification permission in device settings.

Retention, backups and app-data deletion

Saved records remain in local app storage until removed. To remove all local study and notebook information, use your device’s app-data deletion controls or uninstall the app and its data. Offloading an app may retain its data.

Operating-system backups may retain copies according to your device’s backup and restore settings. Those settings control backup copies separately from the app’s Delete selected saved notebook action. Apple or Google retains store purchase history separately.

Younger learners

No account or data-upload service is provided, including for younger learners. A parent or guardian can manage store purchases and device notification and backup settings. Avoid including identifiable learner information in rehearsal notes.
