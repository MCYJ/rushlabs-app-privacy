Level 1 ESOL Action Study

Privacy policy

App reference: glb-2629
Updated November 3, 2026

This policy covers Level 1 ESOL Action Study, a paid independent study app for selected written English topics connected with the ESB Level 1 Award in ESOL Skills for Life (Speaking and Listening). No RushLabs account is required.

Information on your device

The app stores answer history, bookmarks, study progress, study activity and optional local quiz reminder settings in local app storage on your device. Study content is included in the app; normal study does not require a RushLabs server connection.

The action board

The action board can hold a goal, task descriptions, suggested owners, due points, learner-recorded replies and completion, and turn order or status. These entries are study notes. A recorded reply does not verify another person's consent. Use fictional names or initials and avoid entering sensitive personal information.

Save stores a snapshot on this device. Opening the board shows an example draft. Use Reopen to load your saved snapshot. Reset own saved board removes only the board's stored copy and ordinarily restores the unsaved example. If newer edits occur during the reset, the app keeps them visible and unsaved. Study answers and bookmarks are stored separately.

RushLabs does not receive the board contents through the app. There is no shared online workspace or RushLabs-hosted board backup.

Information we do not collect

RushLabs does not collect or receive your answers, bookmarks, study records, board entries or notification content through this app. The app provides no study-data upload service and does not upload contacts, precise location, photos or personal files to RushLabs. This release has no advertising or third-party behavioral analytics.

This policy page contains no advertising, analytics scripts or forms. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process ordinary connection information under GitHub’s privacy statement. Opening an official-source, terms or support link is subject to that destination’s privacy policy.

Purchases

Apple or Google handles the app purchase and download through your store account. One paid download provides the included study content and action board without an additional subscription or in-app purchase. RushLabs does not receive your payment-card details or purchase receipts. Store payment information and purchase history are handled by the store.

Optional local notifications

Quiz reminders are off by default. They are scheduled on your device after you enable them and grant the required device permission. Question text is hidden by default and appears in notifications only after your explicit opt-in. The app uses your device’s time-zone setting to schedule reminders locally. You can disable reminders, hide question text and manage notification permission in device settings.

Retention, backups and app-data deletion

Saved records remain in local app storage until removed. To remove all local study and board information, use your device’s app-data deletion controls or uninstall the app and its data. Offloading an app may retain its data.

Operating-system backups may retain copies according to your device’s backup and restore settings. Apple or Google retains store purchase history separately.

Younger learners

No account or data-upload service is provided, including for younger learners. A parent or guardian can manage store purchases and device notification and backup settings.
