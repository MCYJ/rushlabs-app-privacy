Solo Dance G5 Evidence Study

Privacy policy

App reference: glb-2683

RushLabs develops Solo Dance G5 Evidence Study. This policy describes how the app handles your study information.

Information saved on your device

The app saves study activity and preferences on your device so you can return to your work. The Interpretation Evidence Atlas saves your portfolio details, observations, interpretations, evidence links and unfinished entries in the app’s local storage. Avoid entering information about other people that you do not want to keep on your device.

You can delete the saved Atlas and its current entries using the Atlas deletion control. Your device’s backup settings may determine whether app data is included in a device backup.

Notifications and links

Lock Quiz notifications are optional. You can turn them off in the app or change the app’s notification permissions in iOS Settings. If you choose to show study text on the lock screen, other people who can see the screen may be able to read it.

Opening an external reference or support link takes you to another service. That service handles your visit under its own privacy policy. Apple processes App Store transactions under its own terms. If you contact support, RushLabs uses the information you provide to respond to your request.
